## Fact sheet

- What it is: a web page that opens InfoPath templates (.xsn) and their records (.xml), keeps them in a library in the browser, fills in new records and saves them.
- For whom: the IT admin who has to move the files, the form owner who built them, the auditor who has to read an old record.
- The date: InfoPath Forms Services left SharePoint Online on July 14, 2026.
- Library on the page: 48 forms and 153 records (the forms shipped with the app, all MIT, plus one by Peter Theill).
- Expense Report: 30 records; record 11 is Yara Ingram, E20011, Operations, Site audit, US dollar, five expenses, total $1,271.37.
- Find "site audit": "6 of 30 records match."
- Summary by department: Finance 7, Human Resources 5, Information Technology 6, Operations 5, Sales 7; all 30.
- X-ray of Total: Calculation, sum(xdMath:Nz(../my:expenses/my:expense/my:amount)), in manifest.xsf. X-ray of Amount: Validation "Must be > 0".
- Rule Trace sentences as the app prints them.
- Export file names: 02-expense-report-records.xlsx, 02-expense-report-records.zip, 02-expense-report-archive.zip, expense-report-summary.csv, afterlife-migration-plan.csv, form-documentation.zip, infopath-afterlife-library-2026-11-13.zip.
- Triage: 48 forms, wave 1: 23, wave 2: 17, wave 3: 8. Purchase request: 18 native, 10 emulated, 3 manual, 58% native, three causes.
- Limits: code-behind and ActiveX controls do not run; data connections are listed, never called; the library lives in one browser on one computer.
- Privacy: nothing is uploaded; the page's policy forbids network requests; works offline once loaded; no account.
- Price and licence: free, MIT.

## Departures from the kit, made by the page

- Fonts: Manrope and JetBrains Mono are served from fonts/ (latin subsets, SIL Open Font License), not from the dealt Google Fonts block, so the page asks no other site for anything.
- Palette: ink and muted are darker than the dealt ones, to reach 7:1 on the paper ground.
- Tablets (640 to 1023px): in the tools' tall panels a piece shorter than its panel stands whole in the middle; the kit's to-edge placement stretched it into an empty box.
- Short screens (under 860px of height): a section keeps at least its screens and grows rather than lie over the next one, and the fill and triage grids drop their minimum height.
- Rows a frame cannot show are not drawn (container queries on the tables, the find card on phones).
- One inline style: "X-ray" does not break across lines.
